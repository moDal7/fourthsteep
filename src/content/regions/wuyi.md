knownFor:
  - Da Hong Pao

---

Wuyi is two crafts in one range. On the cliffs, strip-style oolong is bruised, twisted, and charcoal-roasted until mineral “yan yun” shows. In Tongmu, the same mountains produce black tea — pine-dried Xiao Zhong and honeyed Jin Jun Mei.

“Da Hong Pao” on a supermarket tin is often a blend. Zheng yan (true cliff) and cultivar names (Rou Gui, Shui Xian) are the more honest map.
