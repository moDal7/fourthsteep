knownFor:
  - Shincha

---

Kagoshima wins the calendar. Warm volcanic prefecture, early harvest, cultivars bred for colour and umami. Shincha from here is often the first “new tea” in the shops. Deep-steamed lots are common; read the bag for mushi style rather than assuming sencha always looks like a needle.
