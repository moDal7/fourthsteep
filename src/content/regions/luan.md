Lu’an (六安 *Lù'ān*) is not Huangshan and not Lu’an the homophone trap in other romanisations. Gua Pian is the second leaf only: buds and stems removed, then a distinct bake. Earliness is not the prestige marker here the way it is for Long Jing.
