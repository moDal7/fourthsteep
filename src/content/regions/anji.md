Anji is not Xi Hu. The Hangzhou lake and the Anji bamboo hills share a province and a pan-fire idiom; they do not share a garden. An Ji Bai Cha is a green tea whose cultivar leaf is pale — a correction the atlas already makes on the entry, and now makes on the map.
