Anxi taught the world to roll oolong into beads. Modern “jade” Tie Guan Yin is green, orchid-loud, and perishable; traditional roast is darker, warmer, and more patient. The cultivar and the shake-and-kill-green timing matter more than the English name on the pouch.

Gongfu with a rinse to open the bead; boiling water is welcome.
