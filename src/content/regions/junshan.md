洞庭湖 *Dòngtíng Hú* is the Hunan lake. Junshan sits in it. The English name “Dongting” also belongs to the Jiangsu hills that grow Bi Luo Chun — a coincidence the atlas treats as a correction, not a grouping.

Jun Shan Yin Zhen is a yellow tea: kill-green, then 闷黄. It is not a white silver needle that happens to be yellow in the cup, and it is not filed under Hangzhou.
