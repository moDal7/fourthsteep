Fujian is too large to be one terroir. This page gathers the Fuding white-tea coast, where wither-only teas — Silver Needle, White Peony, Shou Mei — are defined by cultivar down and time, not by a wok. Anxi and Wuyi have their own pages. Fuzhou’s jasmine pearls are greens wearing a second craft.
