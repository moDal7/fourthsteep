Uji is where Japanese shading became an art. Cloth or frames starve the leaf of light so theanine stays in the leaf; the result is gyokuro if kneaded, tencha if dried as flakes, matcha if those flakes are stone-milled. Brew cool. Store cold. Named cultivars are a better souvenir than a generic “ceremonial” label.
