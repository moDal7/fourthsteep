Huoshan sits in Lu’an prefecture, which already has a page for Liu An Gua Pian. That tea is a green. This one is a yellow. The county line is the honest pin: 霍山黄芽 is not melon-seed leaf, and it is not Meng Ding wearing an Anhui label.
