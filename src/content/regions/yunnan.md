Yunnan’s large-leaf trees can become sun-dried maocha (the start of puer) or fully oxidised Dian Hong. Village names, harvest season, and whether the leaf was pile-fermented (shou) or left raw (sheng) matter more than the English word “puer.” Young sheng is a bitter-floral tea that wants to age; shou is an earth-cocoa tea that wants a rinse.
