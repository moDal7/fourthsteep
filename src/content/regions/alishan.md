Alishan is a range and a style: ball-rolled, lightly oxidised, barely roasted oolong that drinks like cream and gardenia. Elevation (and night temperature) is the ingredient. Jin Xuan’s natural milkiness is not the same as added flavouring — the lid tells the truth.
