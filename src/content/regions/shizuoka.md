Most Japanese tea still comes from Shizuoka. Asamushi keeps the needle and a clear liquor; fukamushi, born on thicker lowland leaf, steams until the cup turns opaque and spinach-sweet. Neither is “better”; they are different instruments. Shincha from here arrives after Kagoshima’s earliest flush.
