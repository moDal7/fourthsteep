Sayama is the atlas’s Kanto chapter: not the largest Japanese origin, but a stubborn local style. Finishing fires are often stronger, the cup thicker and toastier than a delicate Uji gyokuro. Few export bags say “Sayama” in large type; look for Saitama and Iruma. This page is here because the map of Japan’s tea is not only Uji and Shizuoka.
