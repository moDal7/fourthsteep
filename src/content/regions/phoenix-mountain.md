Dan cong means “single bush”: selections kept for a honey-orchid, magnolia-cream, or other aroma, then made as long, wiry oolong. The names are folk poetry (duck-shit aroma included). The brewing is not: Chaozhou gongfu uses a tiny pot, high leaf, and flash steeps so the perfume does not turn bitter.
