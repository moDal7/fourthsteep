洞庭 *Dòngtíng* here is East and West Dongting, the hills around 太湖 in Jiangsu. The English word is a coincidence with 洞庭湖, the Hunan lake that holds Junshan island. They are not the same place, not the same province, and not the same tea.

Bi Luo Chun is the atlas entry filed here: one bud and one tiny leaf, rolled into spirals, floral from the neighbouring fruit trees. Drink it cooler than a Hangzhou Long Jing, and do not file it under Xi Hu.
