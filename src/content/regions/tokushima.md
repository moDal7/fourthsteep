Tokushima is on the map because Awa bancha is not a footnote to Shizuoka sencha. The leaf is boiled, packed until lactic acid bacteria sour it, then sun-dried. That is a dark-tea process in Japanese clothes, and it needs its own prefecture — not a nationwide `regionNote`.
