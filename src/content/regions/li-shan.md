knownFor:
  - Ultra high-mountain oolong

---

Li Shan sits high enough that tea grows slowly and tastes expensive. The cup should be pear-floral and almost without edge. Brew cooler than Wuyi, store like a green, and be suspicious of low-elevation “Li Shan style.”
