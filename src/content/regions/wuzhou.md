Wuzhou is where Liu Bao is traditionally aged in baskets. The process is kill-green and roll on a dark-tea path, then microbial ageing — not Yunnan sun-dried maocha, and not 渥堆 as practised for shou puer. The atlas files it here so “Wuzhou” is a region, not a leftover string.
