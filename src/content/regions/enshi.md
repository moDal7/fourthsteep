Enshi is not Uji and it is not Xi Hu. The leaf is steamed, as Japanese greens are steamed, then rolled and drunk in a Chinese gaiwan at Chinese temperatures. The atlas files Yu Lu here so the comparison with sencha has a place on the map, and so a Hubei tea is not swallowed by a Zhejiang page.
