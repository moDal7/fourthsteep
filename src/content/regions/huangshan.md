Huangshan is orchid-cane greens and theatrical pressed spears, not Wuyi’s charcoal. Hou Kui needs a tall glass; Mao Feng a gaiwan. Gua Pian is from Lu’an, a different Anhui city, and has its own region page.

Drink these cooler than oolong, and treat harvest week as part of the name.
