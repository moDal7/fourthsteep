Huangshan is orchid-cane greens and theatrical pressed spears, not Wuyi’s charcoal. Hou Kui needs a tall glass; Mao Feng a gaiwan; Gua Pian (from Lu’an in the same province) a slightly hotter pot. They share Anhui humidity and a lighter hand than Fujian oolong.
