Wenshan’s Pinglin still makes strip-style Baozhong, the lightest traditional Taiwanese oolong, historically wrapped in paper. A little further toward Hsinchu and Miaoli, summer leafhoppers create Oriental Beauty’s muscat honey. Same island, opposite crafts: perfume-green versus dessert-amber.
