Anhua is the Hunan seat of 黑茶 as a class, not as a translation problem. Fu Zhuan grown and flowered here is not puer, and it is not a light oolong that happens to sit at oxidation 16. Qian Liang is the same family packed as a bamboo 花卷 log. Jingyang in Shaanxi is a sibling tradition; the atlas notes it on the Fu brick, and keeps the map pin on Anhua.
