Before Taiwan’s high-mountain jade style took the export stage, Nantou’s Lugu township was already rolling and roasting oolong. Dong Ding should taste of caramel and osmanthus, not of unroasted gardenia — if it tastes like Alishan, it may be wearing the wrong mountain’s name.
