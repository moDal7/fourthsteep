Qimen is a county in Huangshan prefecture, but it is not Huangshan green-tea country. The leaf here is fully oxidised 红茶 — English black tea, named in Chinese for the liquor. Grades are set per producer and do not compare across vendors.

The atlas files Qimen here so a Keemun is not an unmapped string, and so it is not silently absorbed into the Huangshan greens.
