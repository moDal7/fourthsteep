A listing that wants to be taken seriously names **harvest date, cultivar, elevation or garden, producer, and storage**. Not all five every time — a Qimen factory blend will not have an elevation the way Li Shan does — but the *kind* of fact should match the *kind* of tea.

**Absence is information.**

- A “Ming Qian” Long Jing with no year is a vibe.
- A “ceremonial grade” matcha with no mill date is a colour.
- A “gushu sheng” with no village and no year is a story.
- A “Zheng Yan” Da Hong Pao at a supermarket price is a blend, which is allowed, but then it should say blend.

What to do with the atlas: the quality markers on each entry are written as claims you can test (leaf shape, liquor colour, a named aftertaste). The faults are cause and tell. If a shop cannot survive those tests, the page has done its job.

A [method matrix](./matrix) marks which brewing styles are suitable, workable, or wrong for each tea in the atlas — from the same records as the timer.
