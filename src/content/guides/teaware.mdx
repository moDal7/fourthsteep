You can drink every tea in this atlas with a **gaiwan, a pitcher, and a cup**. Everything else is optimisation.

## The useful few

- **Gaiwan (110–150 ml):** greens, whites, oolongs, tasting yancha. Lid aroma is a diagnostic.
- **Kyusu:** sencha and friends; the side handle and mesh are not decorative.
- **Shiboridashi or tiny pot:** gyokuro.
- **Yixing or Chaozhou clay:** roasted Wuyi, dan cong, puer. One clay pot per family of teas if you believe in seasoning; one pot if you believe in washing.
- **Chawan and chasen:** matcha only.
- **Tall glass:** Hou Kui, Yin Zhen, grandpa greens.

## Heat

Preheat whatever will hold the infusion. A cold thick clay pot is a hidden temperature drop — the schedule on the tea page assumes you did this.

## What not to buy first

Electric gaiwan robots, “daoist” peach-wood, and a shelf of unused chawan. A kettle with a thermometer (or a cheap IR gun) will improve more teas than a second pot.
