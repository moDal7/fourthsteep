The most-asked question on any tea site. Answer it once, correctly, and stop before health.

**Processing class is a poor predictor.** Green versus black tells you very little.

**What actually dominates:** leaf-to-water ratio, steep time, water temperature, and leaf grade. A strong green out-caffeinates a weak black comfortably.

**“White tea is low caffeine” is false.** Bai Hao Yin Zhen is all buds, and buds are the caffeine-rich part of the plant.

**Matcha delivers more than an infusion** of comparable leaf because you consume the leaf rather than an extract.

**“Discard a 30-second first steep to decaffeinate” does not work well.** Only a modest fraction leaves in that window. No percentage; the figures disagree.

This atlas does not publish milligram figures. They vary by an order of magnitude across sources and preparations. It does not extend any of this into health claims.
