Every tea in the world is a sequence drawn from a small closed vocabulary of verbs. That is the actual definition of the six classes.

| Verb | Native | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pluck | 采摘 | Parameter: bud only, one-and-one, one-and-two, open leaf |
| Shade | 覆い | Starve light; theanine stays, catechins drop |
| Wither | 萎凋 | Indoor, outdoor, sun. Long wither *is* the white-tea process |
| Shake | 摇青 / 浪青 | The oolong craft |
| Kill-green | 杀青 | Wok, steam, drum, or basket. Halts enzymatic oxidation |
| Roll | 揉捻 | Strip, bead, ball, spiral, flattened. Matcha never gets this |
| Yellow | 闷黄 | Wrap warm. The whole of the yellow class |
| Oxidise | 发酵 | Enzymatic. The Chinese word means “ferment” — origin of most of the confusion in tea English |
| Pile-ferment | 渥堆 | Microbial, wet, hot, weeks |
| Dry | 干燥 / 晒青 | Oven, charcoal, or **sun**. Sun-drying is what makes puer ageable |
| Roast | 焙火 | After the tea is already a tea |
| Press | 压制 | Cake, brick, tuo |
| Scent | 窨制 | Jasmine, osmanthus |
| Refine | 精制 / 仕上げ | Cutting, sorting, final firing |
| Mill | 碾 | Stone mill. Tencha → matcha |

The Chinese word 发酵 *fājiào* means “ferment.” English tea writing split it into oxidation (enzymatic) and fermentation (microbial). The glossary for [oxidation](../glossary#oxidation) draws the line; the homepage chart now has a second axis so Shou Puer is not a short bar next to a Wuyi oolong.

## Diff the chains

Each tea page that has a compare target also diffs the two processing sequences. Shared verbs stay quiet; the split is the point. Four flagship pairs live on [Processing chains](./chains).

- **Gyokuro and matcha** share shade and steam. They diverge at rolling. Matcha’s chain never includes 揉捻.
- **Sheng and shou** are identical until 渥堆.
- **Long Jing and Bi Luo Chun** differ mainly in shaping: flattened sword versus spiral.
- **Every yellow tea** is a green tea plus 闷黄.
- **Hojicha** is a finished sencha (or bancha) plus 焙火 — which is why its oxidation is 0 and its cup is brown.
